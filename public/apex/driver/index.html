User Flows

1. First Launch And Routing

The app determines whether the driver is signed out, partially activated, pending review, blocked, or ready to drive.

  1. M01 Splash checks session and driver eligibility.
  2. Signed-out drivers go to M02 Sign In and M03 Verification Code.
  3. Unactivated drivers route to M04-M14 based on the next required activation step.
  4. Approved drivers land on M15 Offline Map.

2. Driver Activation

The driver completes identity, vehicle, document, consent, and terms requirements before receiving ride requests.

  1. M05 shows the activation checklist and current step status.
  2. M06-M11 collect identity, license, background consent, vehicle, insurance, and terms.
  3. M12 holds the driver in review while checks are pending.
  4. M13 handles rejected documents with a specific reason and resubmit action.
  5. M14 confirms activation and sends the driver to Drive.

3. Go Online

The driver moves from offline to online only when location, vehicle, account, and document requirements pass.

  1. M15 shows the offline map and Go Online action.
  2. M16 verifies permissions, vehicle eligibility, and account status.
  3. M17 blocks the driver with the exact reason and fix path when requirements fail.
  4. M18 confirms the driver is online and waiting for requests.

4. Ride Request Decision

One request template handles standard, upfront, long-trip, multi-stop, and bonus variants without separate top-level screens.

  1. M19 presents fare, pickup distance, trip estimate, rider confidence, and variant badges.
  2. Decline returns to M21 as a lightweight toast state.
  3. Expired, missed, or unavailable-after-accept outcomes also resolve through M21.
  4. Accept moves to M20 and then the pickup route.

5. Pickup

The pickup flow keeps navigation primary while routing contact, cancellation, pin issues, and no-show logic into supporting states.

  1. M24 guides the driver to pickup.
  2. M25 exposes pickup details, pin issue reporting, contact, and cancel actions.
  3. M26 supports masked call and quick replies.
  4. M27 shows arrived/waiting timing and no-show eligibility.
  5. M28 confirms the correct rider or PIN before starting.
  6. M29-M31 cover cancellation, no-show, and rider-cancelled outcomes.

6. Active Trip

The active trip flow keeps navigation focused while surfacing safety, route issues, connectivity degradation, and trip completion.

  1. M32 is the main in-trip navigation state.
  2. M33 contains trip details, route issue entry, early end, and support paths.
  3. M34 gives in-trip safety access.
  4. M35 handles destination changes.
  5. M36 handles degraded GPS or connectivity.
  6. M37-M38 confirm dropoff and complete the ride.

7. Post-Trip

After completion, the driver can rate, review earnings, report an issue, or return online.

  1. M39 confirms the trip is finished and earnings are processing.
  2. M40 collects rider rating and only asks for reasons when needed.
  3. M41 summarizes fare, bonus, fees, and total earnings.
  4. M42 routes trip issues into the unified support model.
  5. M43 lets the driver return online or end the session.

8. Earnings And Payouts

Earnings stay intentionally compact: today summary, trip list, trip detail, cash out, and payout method.

  1. M44 shows today earnings and safe driving bonus eligibility.
  2. M45 lists completed trips.
  3. M46 shows fare detail for one trip and links to review.
  4. M47 confirms instant cash out amount and fee.
  5. M48 manages payout destination.

9. Account, Vehicle, Documents, Settings

Account is the home for driver identity, vehicles, document health, settings, safety, and help.

  1. M49 is the account menu and includes the safe driving score entry.
  2. M50-M51 manage active and ineligible vehicles.
  3. M52-M55 use one document pattern for approved, reviewing, rejected, expiring, and blocked states.
  4. M56-M58 cover settings, navigation app choice, and notifications.

10. Safe Driving Score

GPS-derived safety scoring is visible but not overly forensic, with enough detail for trust and bonus eligibility.

  1. M59 shows the top-level safe driving score.
  2. The score breaks down speed limit respected, stopping at stop signs, not swerving, and dangerous driving score.
  3. Green/red eligibility indicates whether the driver qualifies for the safe driving bonus.
  4. M44, M49, and M60 provide entry points into the score.

11. Safety And Emergency

Safety actions stay reachable from Drive, Pickup, Active Trip, Account, and Help.

  1. M60 is the safety hub.
  2. M61 prioritizes emergency assistance and current location context.
  3. M62 collects safety issue reports.
  4. Emergency actions should never be disabled.

12. Help And Support

One support model handles fares, rider issues, lost items, damage, route problems, cancellation, and safety issues.

  1. M63 starts with search and support categories.
  2. M64 lets the driver pick a recent trip when the issue is trip-specific.
  3. M65 routes the issue category.
  4. M66 captures issue details and optional evidence.
  5. M67 keeps the support case conversation in one thread.
